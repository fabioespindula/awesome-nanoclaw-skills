# Challenge Round

Use this reference only when multiagent or swarm execution is available and the decision deserves extra rigor.

The challenge round should turn five separate perspectives into useful disagreement. It is optional for short, chat-first answers.

## Process

1. Remove advisor names from the first-pass outputs.
2. Give each advisor the anonymized outputs from the others.
3. Ask each advisor to identify:
   - the strongest point made by someone else;
   - the weakest assumption in the set;
   - the disagreement that should affect the recommendation.
4. Do not let advisors rewrite the final answer. Use the challenge round as synthesis input only.

## Prompt

Read the anonymized board perspectives below.

Your job is not to defend your original view. Identify what should change in the final recommendation.

Return:

- Strongest outside point
- Weakest assumption
- Disagreement that matters
- Change you would make to the recommendation

Keep the answer concise and stay in the user's dominant language.
