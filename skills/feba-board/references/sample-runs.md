# Sample Runs

These examples show the intended tone and size. Do not treat them as fixed scripts.

## Portuguese Decision Review

User:

```txt
/board Devo fazer launch agora ou esperar melhorar onboarding?
```

Assistant:

```md
**FEBA Board**
Decision: lancar agora com onboarding imperfeito ou esperar para melhorar a primeira experiencia.

Recommendation: lance agora para um grupo controlado, nao para o publico inteiro.

Board view:
- The Skeptic: o risco e que um onboarding ruim queime confianca e gere churn precoce.
- The Thesis: a tese boa e aprender com usuarios reais, mas so se voce medir a friccao de entrada.
- The Market: esperar pode atrasar distribuicao; um beta controlado preserva timing sem fingir que esta pronto.
- The Customer: o usuario tolera imperfeicao se o valor inicial for claro e o suporte for rapido.
- The Operator: defina um cohort pequeno, um checklist de ativacao e um criterio de pausa.

Tension: o upside de aprender agora e real, mas so funciona se voce limitar o blast radius.
Next action: abrir para 10-20 usuarios ideais e medir ativacao, tempo ate valor e motivos de abandono.

Reply with `expand <advisor>`, `compare options`, `make a plan`, or `save transcript`.
```

## English Investment Review

User:

```txt
/board Pressure-test this acquisition thesis before we send the memo.
```

Assistant:

```md
**FEBA Board**
Decision: whether the acquisition thesis is strong enough to send the memo now.

Recommendation: send a sharper memo only after tightening the customer and distribution assumptions.

Board view:
- The Skeptic: the downside is buying growth that depends on channels you do not control.
- The Thesis: the memo needs one crisp claim about why this asset compounds inside FEBA.
- The Market: timing may be good if the category is consolidating and sellers are still rational.
- The Customer: validate that customers buy for durable workflow value, not temporary discounts or relationships.
- The Operator: add a 30-day diligence plan with owner, proof point, and kill criteria.

Tension: the market window may reward speed, but weak customer proof makes speed expensive.
Next action: add three diligence questions that can invalidate the deal before partner review.

Reply with `expand <advisor>`, `compare options`, `make a plan`, or `save transcript`.
```
