# Sample Runs

These examples show the intended tone and size. Do not treat them as fixed scripts.

## Launch Decision Review

User:

```txt
/febaboard Should we launch now or wait until onboarding is better?
```

Assistant:

```md
**FEBA Board**
Decision: launch now with imperfect onboarding or wait to improve the first experience.

Recommendation: launch now to a controlled group, not to the full public audience.

Board view:
- The Skeptic: poor onboarding can burn trust and create early churn.
- The Thesis: learning from real users is valuable only if you measure entry friction.
- The Market: waiting can delay distribution; a controlled beta preserves timing without pretending the product is ready.
- The Customer: users tolerate imperfection when initial value is clear and support is fast.
- The Operator: define a small cohort, an activation checklist, and a pause criterion.

Tension: the upside of learning now is real, but it only works if you limit the blast radius.
Next action: open to 10-20 ideal users and measure activation, time to value, and abandonment reasons.

Reply with `expand <advisor>`, `compare options`, `make a plan`, or `save transcript`.
```

## English Investment Review

User:

```txt
/febaboard Pressure-test this acquisition thesis before we send the memo.
```

Assistant:

```md
**FEBA Board**
Decision: whether the acquisition thesis is strong enough to send the memo now.

Recommendation: send a sharper memo only after tightening the customer and distribution assumptions.

Board view:
- The Skeptic: the downside is buying growth that depends on channels you do not control.
- The Thesis: the memo needs one crisp claim about why this asset compounds inside FEBA.
- The Market: timing may be good if the category is consolidating and sellers are still rational.
- The Customer: validate that customers buy for durable workflow value, not temporary discounts or relationships.
- The Operator: add a 30-day diligence plan with owner, proof point, and kill criteria.

Tension: the market window may reward speed, but weak customer proof makes speed expensive.
Next action: add three diligence questions that can invalidate the deal before partner review.

Reply with `expand <advisor>`, `compare options`, `make a plan`, or `save transcript`.
```
